## About Me

Welcome to my site!

I am a Senior Economist in the Real Sector Stability (RSS) Division of the Financial Stability Department (FSD) at the Bank of Canada. My research focuses on housing, household finance, urban and public economics, with an emphasis on questions relevant to economic policy. My work has examined housing affordability and land-use regulation, wealth inequality, access to financial services, and the fiscal impacts of immigration.

I received my PhD in Economics from the University of Toronto in 2024. Before joining the Bank of Canada, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Alberta School of Business and also worked with the Bridging Divides project and the School of Cities at the University of Toronto.
